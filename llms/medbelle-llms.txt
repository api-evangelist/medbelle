## What Medbelle did

- Founded 2016 in London by Leander de Laporte and Daniel Kolb.
- Operated a technology platform (Medbelle OS) plus human Patient Care Advisers to route
  patients through consultation, diagnostics, surgery and aftercare.
- CareConnect was its virtual-first specialist-assessment and pathway-guidance service, sold
  to hospitals, insurers, employers and case managers.
- Specialties included cosmetic, bariatric, orthopaedic and ophthalmic surgery.
